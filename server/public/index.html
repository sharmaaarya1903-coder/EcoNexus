Do's & Don'ts

Practical choices to reduce microfiber release.

✅ Do

  • Prefer durable, lower-shedding fabrics when practical.
  • Wash clothes only when needed.
  • Use a microfiber-catching laundry filter or bag where appropriate.
  • Follow the garment care label.
  • Choose quality garments designed to last.

❌ Don't

  • Wash every garment after a single light use.
  • Overload the washing machine.
  • Ignore care instructions.
  • Treat an estimate as laboratory-tested product data.
  • Throw away usable clothing just because it contains synthetic fibres.
EcoNexus focuses on informed choices, not blaming people for using synthetic clothing.

Why It Matters

A simple introduction to microfiber pollution.

🌊

From clothes to water

Synthetic textiles can release tiny plastic fibres during washing. These fibres can enter wastewater and contribute to plastic pollution.

The goal of EcoNexus is not to label every synthetic garment as “bad”, but to give people clearer information so they can make more informed choices.
